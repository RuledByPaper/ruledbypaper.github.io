Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 August 2026

RuledByPaper is a one-person shop that sells printable calendar and planner files. This page explains what happens to personal information when you buy from us, download a free file, or interact with our Pinterest account.

Who we are

RuledByPaper is operated by an individual seller based in South Korea. We do not run our own website or checkout. Our products are sold and delivered through Gumroad.

Contact: [foundinmargin@gmail.com]

Where your information goes when you buy or download

All purchases and free downloads happen on Gumroad, not on a site we operate. Gumroad acts as the merchant of record. When you buy a product or request a free download, Gumroad collects and processes the information needed to complete it — typically your email address, and for paid orders your payment and billing details.

We never see or store your payment details. Those are handled entirely by Gumroad and its payment providers.

Gumroad does show us, in our seller dashboard, the email address and order details of people who buy or download our files. We use this only to:

  • deliver the files you asked for
  • answer questions about an order
  • tell customers about product updates or new releases, where Gumroad's own tools are used to send that message and you can unsubscribe at any time

We do not sell, rent, or share customer information with anyone else, and we do not use it for advertising.

Gumroad's own privacy policy governs what Gumroad does with your data: gumroad.com/privacy

Pinterest

We publish pins for our own products to our own Pinterest account, using Pinterest's official API through an application registered to this shop.

That application only posts content we created to our own account. It does not read, collect, or store information about any other Pinterest user, and it is not used on behalf of anyone else. The access token it uses is stored on the shop owner's own computer and is not shared.

What Pinterest itself collects when you browse Pinterest is governed by Pinterest's privacy policy.

Our files

The PDFs we sell contain no tracking, no scripts, and no hidden identifiers. They do not report anything back to us when you open or print them.

Cookies and analytics

We do not operate a website, so we set no cookies and run no analytics of our own. Gumroad and Pinterest each set their own cookies on their own sites, under their own policies linked above.

Children

Our products are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Your choices

Because the data lives with Gumroad rather than with us, requests to see, correct, or delete your information are usually fastest through Gumroad. You can also contact us at the address above and we will help.

If you no longer want emails from us, use the unsubscribe link in any message.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.
